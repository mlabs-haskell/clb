# Contributing to CLB

Thank you for your interest in contributing to CLB! We value, and welcome,
all contributions, whether you are a downstream user of CLB, or just
interested in the project and using it to develop your skills or do something
fun or interesting.

For details on how to set up a development environment, see [our
documentation](https://mlabs-haskell.github.io/clb-docs/build). 

If you have any questions, don’t hesitate to reach out through the support
channels listed in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md).
